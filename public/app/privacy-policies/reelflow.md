## 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

### 1. 核心理念 / Our Philosophy

**ReelFlow** 是一款以本地工作流为核心的 macOS 应用。我们认为，你导入的照片、音频和导出结果首先属于你自己。
**ReelFlow** is a local-first macOS app. We believe the photos, audio files, and exports you work with belong to you.

### 2. 数据收集与存储 / Data Collection & Storage

* **本地处理 (Local Processing)**：你导入的照片、音频和导出配置主要用于本地预览、本地渲染和本地导出。
* **无需账户 (No Accounts)**：当前版本不要求注册、登录，也不会默认收集你的姓名、邮箱或其他个人身份信息。
* **本地诊断信息 (Local Diagnostics)**：当你主动导出诊断包时，应用可能包含本地错误摘要、日志和配置快照，用于帮助定位导出问题。
* **Local-first workflow**: imported media and export settings are primarily used for local preview and local rendering.
* **No account required**: the app does not require sign-up and does not intentionally collect personal identity information by default.
* **Local diagnostics**: if you choose to export a diagnostics bundle, it may contain local logs, failure summaries, and configuration snapshots for troubleshooting.

### 3. 不会默认做的事 / What We Do Not Do by Default

* **不默认上传素材 (No automatic cloud upload)**：应用不会默认把你的照片、音频或导出结果上传到云端。
* **不默认行为追踪 (No default tracking)**：当前版本不以内置广告平台或第三方行为追踪为核心能力。
* **不出售个人数据 (No sale of personal data)**：我们不会把你导入的本地素材出售给第三方。
* **No automatic upload** of your media or exports by default.
* **No ad-tech style tracking** as a core product behavior in the current version.
* **No sale of your local content** to third parties.

### 4. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

ReelFlow 的不同发行版本可能会通过苹果提供的服务完成分发、支付或交易校验，例如 **Mac App Store / StoreKit**。在这种情况下，支付、交易与账户信息由苹果处理，开发者无法访问你的银行卡信息等敏感支付数据。
Some ReelFlow distributions may rely on Apple services such as the **Mac App Store / StoreKit** for distribution or purchase validation. In those cases, Apple handles payment and account data; the developer does not receive your sensitive financial information.
